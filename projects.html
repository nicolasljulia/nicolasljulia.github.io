Where is Nico

This site. A hand-built, low-poly Earth that shows where I am in the world right now. The continents and country outlines come from real Natural Earth border data, turned into a stylised 3D globe in Blender, then brought to the web with three.js.

The little character waving on the surface is me. His position and the speech bubble above him are live: an automation on my phone sends my location to this site's GitHub repo, and the globe reads it and moves him there. There are also cargo ships and a sailboat crossing the oceans, two biplanes trailing contrails, and a moon, because a globe should have company.
